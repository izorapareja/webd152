About Pacific Trails Resort

At Pacific Trails Resort, we believe a memorable getaway begins with slowing down and appreciating nature. Our vision is to create a peaceful coastal retreat where guests can step away from their busy routines, breathe in the ocean air, and enjoy the beauty of California's North Coast. From our comfortable yurts to the breathtaking views of the Pacific Ocean, we want every visit to feel relaxing, welcoming, and unforgettable.

Our team values thoughtful hospitality, respect for nature, and meaningful experiences. We want our guests to feel at home while discovering everything the coast has to offer. Whether it is hiking scenic trails, kayaking, enjoying delicious meals, or simply watching the sunset from a private deck, there is something for everyone. Pacific Trails Resort is more than a place to stay. It is a place to reconnect with nature, create lasting memories, and enjoy life's quieter moments.

What We Value

  • Welcoming hospitality that makes every guest feel at home
  • Thoughtfully prepared meals and memorable dining experiences
  • Peaceful spaces designed for rest and relaxation
  • Opportunities to explore and appreciate the beauty of the coast
