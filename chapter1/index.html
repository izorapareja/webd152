My favorite beverage is pumpkin spice coffee. I love drinking it because it reminds me of fall, which is my favorite season. The warm and sweet spices makes me feel cozy and gives me a lot of comfort. It also reminds me of Over the Garden Wall, which is my favorite show to watch during the fall season. I usually buy my pumpkin spice coffee from Starbucks, but I also enjoy making my own at home. It's something that we don't have in the Philippines so it's a new taste that I have grown to love here in the US and I love recommending it to my friends
